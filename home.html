SDG 14 · Life Below Water

Protect Life
Below Water

Our oceans cover 71% of the Earth's surface and support billions of lives. SDG 14 calls on all of us to conserve and sustainably use marine and coastal ecosystems — because a healthy ocean means a healthy planet.

Why SDG 14 Matters

The ocean is not just beautiful — it is essential. Here is what is at stake, and what you can do.

Marine Biodiversity

Over 2 million species live in our oceans. Pollution, overfishing, and climate change threaten their survival and the ecosystems that sustain all life on Earth.

  • Reduce single-use plastics in your daily routine
  • Support sustainable seafood choices & certifications
  • Participate in local beach or river clean-ups

Ocean & Climate

The ocean absorbs 25% of all CO₂ emissions and generates 50% of Earth's oxygen. Protecting it is not optional — it is central to tackling the climate crisis we all face.

  • Lower your carbon footprint through everyday choices
  • Advocate for renewable energy in your community
  • Share verified ocean-climate facts on social media

Sustainable Fisheries

34% of fish stocks are currently overexploited. Sustainable fishing protects livelihoods for 600 million people who depend on fisheries worldwide for food and income.

  • Choose MSC-certified fish when shopping
  • Report suspicious or illegal fishing activity
  • Support fishing communities & fair trade seafood
